The light cooking guide

Cooking methods, pantry staples and flavour tricks for meals that are lighter but still full of flavour.

Methods

Cooking methods compared

The way you cook makes as much difference as what you cook. These methods use little added fat and keep ingredients tasting fresh.

MethodGreat forWhy it works
SteamingBroccoli, green beans, asparagus, fishKeeps colour and crunch; no added fat needed
RoastingRoot vegetables, peppers, cauliflower, chickenCaramelises for deep flavour with a little oil
GrillingCourgette, peppers, fish, lean meatSmoky flavour; fat drips away
Stir-fryingPeppers, greens, mushrooms, tofuFast, high heat, minimal oil
PoachingEggs, fish, chickenGentle and moist; flavour the liquid with herbs
Baking in parcelsFish and vegetablesSteams in its own juices with herbs and lemon
Flavour

Big flavour, less salt

  • Acid: a squeeze of lemon or lime brightens almost any dish.
  • Herbs: add fresh herbs at the end for colour and aroma.
  • Spices: toast cumin, coriander or paprika for depth.
  • Umami: mushrooms, tomatoes, soy sauce and Parmesan add savoury richness.
  • Heat: a pinch of chilli wakes everything up.
Pantry

A lighter pantry

Whole grains

Oats, brown rice, quinoa, wholewheat pasta and bread.

Beans & lentils

Tinned and dried, for salads, soups and stews.

Frozen veg

Peas, spinach, broccoli and mixed vegetables.

Flavour boosters

Olive oil, lemons, garlic, herbs, spices and stock.
