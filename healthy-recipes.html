Rainbow grain bowl with tahini dressing

Ingredients

  • 100 g quinoa or brown rice
  • 1 sweet potato, cubed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small red cabbage wedge, shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2–3 tbsp water

Method

  1. Roast the sweet potato with the oil at 200°C (400°F) for 20 minutes.
  2. Cook the quinoa or rice according to the pack.
  3. Whisk the tahini, lemon juice and water until smooth and pourable.
  4. Arrange the grains, vegetables, chickpeas and avocado in bowls and drizzle with dressing.

Tip: Cook a double batch of grains for quick lunches all week.

Wholewheat pasta primavera

Ingredients

  • 300 g wholewheat pasta
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 courgette, sliced
  • 150 g asparagus or green beans
  • 150 g peas
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan
  • Handful of basil

Method

  1. Cook the pasta, adding the asparagus and peas for the last 3 minutes. Save a cup of cooking water.
  2. Sauté the courgette and garlic in the oil for 4 minutes.
  3. Toss everything together with lemon, a splash of pasta water and Parmesan.
  4. Finish with basil and black pepper.

Tip: Pasta water makes a light, silky sauce without cream.

Green goddess salad

Ingredients

  • 1 head romaine or butter lettuce
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 avocado
  • Handful of basil and parsley
  • 100 g Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 2 tbsp toasted seeds

Method

  1. Blend the yogurt, half the avocado, herbs, lemon juice and garlic until smooth; season.
  2. Chop the lettuce and cucumber and slice the remaining avocado.
  3. Toss with the dressing and scatter over the seeds.

Tip: The dressing keeps for two days in the fridge and doubles as a dip.

Breakfast fruit and yogurt plate

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain Greek yogurt
  • Seasonal fruit such as berries, kiwi, orange and melon
  • 2 tbsp oats or granola
  • 1 tbsp chopped nuts
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Method

  1. Spoon the yogurt onto plates or into bowls.
  2. Slice the fruit and arrange it around the yogurt.
  3. Sprinkle with oats, nuts and cinnamon and drizzle with a little honey if you like.

Tip: Unsweetened yogurt with naturally sweet fruit is a simple everyday swap.

Greek chickpea salad

Ingredients

  • 2 × 400 g tins chickpeas, drained
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ red onion, finely chopped
  • Handful of olives
  • 100 g feta
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Method

  1. Combine the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, onion and olives.
  2. Whisk the oil, lemon juice and oregano with salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the salad with the dressing and crumble over the feta.

Tip: It gets even better after an hour in the fridge, perfect for packed lunches.

One-pan lemon chicken and vegetables

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs or breasts
  • 500 g baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 red pepper, in chunks
  • 1 courgette, in chunks
  • 1 red onion, in wedges
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Toss the potatoes and vegetables with half the oil and seasoning in a large tray; roast for 10 minutes.
  3. Rub the chicken with the remaining oil, lemon, garlic and oregano, add to the tray and roast for 25 minutes more.
  4. Check the chicken reaches 74°C (165°F), then squeeze over extra lemon to serve.

Tip: Use plenty of space on the tray so the vegetables roast instead of steaming.
